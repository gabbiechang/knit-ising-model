## [0.1.0] - knitted samples version

**This is the version of the code used to generate the patterns for the
physical knitted samples.** 

The knitted patterns were generated with Metropolis and Wolff Monte Carlo
on a 100 x 100 periodic lattice and exported as PNGs (black = knit,
white = purl) with a 20-stitch rice-stitch border and 20 rows of knit at the
top and bottom, then knitted on a Kniterate machine.

The exact PNG files that were knitted are in `sample_knits/`, alongside their
physical swatch.
